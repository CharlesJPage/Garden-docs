# Garden Guide Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 1, 2026
**Last updated:** May 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Garden Guide collects, how it's used, and your rights as a user. Garden Guide is a personal gardening recommendation app currently in beta testing through Apple TestFlight.

This app is developed by **Charles Page** as an individual developer. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact: **pageling2+gg@gmail.com**.

---

## What Garden Guide collects

**Account information.** When you sign up, Garden Guide collects your email address and a password. The password is never stored in plain text — it is hashed and managed by Supabase, the authentication and database service Garden Guide uses.

**Garden data you enter.** Garden Guide stores the information you create in the app, including:

- Garden name, location, and hardiness zone
- Beds (name, type, size, irrigation zone)
- Plantings (species, variety, quantity, planting dates, growth stage, notes)
- Observations you log (pest sightings, plant conditions, weather)
- Task completion records (when you marked tasks done or skipped)

This is the data the app needs to give you recommendations. It is associated with your account and not visible to other users.

**Crash and error reports.** If the app crashes or encounters an error, Garden Guide uses **Sentry** to collect technical information that helps fix the problem. This includes device model, operating system version, app version, and a record of the actions leading up to the crash. Crash reports may include your account's user ID so I can correlate reports across sessions, but they do not include your password, the contents of your garden data, or any photos.

**What Garden Guide does not collect.** Garden Guide does not include analytics, advertising, location tracking beyond the location text you optionally enter for your garden, contact list access, or social media integrations. There is no tracking of how you use the app feature-by-feature.

---

## How information is used

The information Garden Guide collects is used only to:

- Provide the core functionality of the app — accounts, recommendations, task tracking
- Diagnose and fix crashes and errors
- Communicate with you about your account if necessary (for example, password resets)

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. There is no advertising in Garden Guide.

---

## Who has access to your data

**Supabase** hosts the Garden Guide database and handles authentication. Your account information and garden data are stored on Supabase's infrastructure. Supabase's privacy practices are described at https://supabase.com/privacy.

**Sentry** receives crash and error reports. Sentry's privacy practices are described at https://sentry.io/privacy/.

**Apple** may collect information through TestFlight separately from Garden Guide, including crash logs and feedback you submit through the TestFlight app. Apple's TestFlight privacy practices are described in Apple's privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

No other third parties have access to your data. The app developer (Charles Page) has access to the Supabase database for development and support purposes.

---

## Where data is stored

Garden Guide's Supabase database is hosted in the United States. By using Garden Guide, you understand that your data will be stored and processed in the United States.

---

## Your rights

You can:

- **Access your data** by viewing it in the app at any time.
- **Correct your data** by editing it in the app.
- **Delete your account and all associated data** by emailing pageling2+gg@gmail.com. Account deletion is processed manually during the beta period; allow up to 7 days. After deletion, your data is removed from the active database. Backups containing deleted data are retained for up to 30 days before being purged.
- **Export your data** by emailing pageling2+gg@gmail.com to request a copy. This is also a manual process during beta.

If you are in a region with additional rights under laws like GDPR or CCPA, those rights apply, and you can exercise them by contacting the email above.

---

## Data retention

Account information and garden data are retained while your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is deleted as described above.

Crash reports in Sentry are retained for 30 days, then automatically deleted.

---

## Children

Garden Guide is not directed at children under 13, and Charles Page does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to Garden Guide, please contact pageling2+gg@gmail.com so the account can be deleted.

Garden Guide is currently distributed through Apple TestFlight as a beta product. Beta software may contain bugs and may behave unexpectedly. Crash reporting is enabled to help identify and fix issues. By participating in the beta, you understand that crash data and error reports will be collected as described above.
